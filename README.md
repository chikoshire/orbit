## Why Orbit exists

Most companies do not have a software problem. They have an **operating system problem**.

Tools multiply. Data fragments. Handoffs leak. AI gets bolted on as chat instead of being governed as part of execution.

Orbit is the productised answer we built after living that pain inside a service business — then shipping the same formula for clients through TUXX.

```text
Leads → Qualification → Jobs / Work → Scheduling → Execution → QA → Billing → Insight
         └─────────────── one system of record + agent layer ───────────────┘
```

## What Orbit is

| Layer | Role |
| --- | --- |
| **Operations hub** | Unified portal for scheduling, tasks, data, reporting, and communication |
| **Workflow engine** | Multi-stage pipelines with rules, SLAs, and role-aware routing |
| **Data model** | Structured entities for clients, jobs, teams, compliance, and outcomes |
| **Orion** | Cognitive / commercial execution layer — intent → governed action (not a model router) |
| **Agent surface** | Scoped agents for scheduling, compliance, reporting, routing, and ops assist |

Orbit is **B2B SaaS** oriented around **lead-to-launched** and **service-operations** workflows — field teams, facilities, commercial cleaning, logistics, building services, and similarly complex ops.

## Product principles

1. **Own the control plane** — AI runs *on* your system, not instead of one  
2. **Systems over features** — routes, rules, and schemas before widgets  
3. **Local-first where it matters** — Orion is designed as a governor, not a cloud proxy for personal subscriptions  
4. **Human capability up** — automation removes repetition; people keep judgment  
5. **Private by design** — production code, prompts, runbooks, and client data stay private  

## Who it is for

- Field service & technician teams with SLAs and revisits  
- Property & facilities operations across sites  
- Commercial cleaning and multi-site dispatch  
- Logistics / fleet-style operational complexity  
- Multi-stage trade and building-services workflows  
- Regulated ops that need audit trails and quality checks

## Stack (high level)

Public-safe summary of the product surface:

- **Web / product UI** — modern TypeScript application layer  
- **Native companions** — macOS / desktop surfaces in the Orbit family  
- **Backend & data** — structured operational data with role-based access  
- **Automation & agents** — Orion-governed execution, not ad-hoc chatbot wrappers  
- **Integrations** — selective connectors (docs and sandbox examples published separately where safe)
